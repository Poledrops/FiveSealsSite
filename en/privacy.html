Privacy and Data Protection

This page explains plainly what happens to your data while you play Five Seals and browse this website. The short answer: the game never sends any personal data to us, because it does not even have permission to access the internet.

Effective 15 November 2026 Last updated 15 November 2026 Version 1.0

No internet permission

The game asks Android for one permission only: vibration. It cannot connect to a network or send data.

Your data stays on your device

Progress, settings and save slots live only in the app's private storage on your phone.

No accounts, ads or analytics

You never need to sign up. No advertising, tracking or analytics tools are used.

No cookies

This website uses no cookies, local storage or tracking pixels. Even the fonts are served from our own server.

Scope and purpose

This notice explains how personal data is processed in connection with the Five Seals mobile game (Android package name com.cypnos.fiveseals) and this website. It serves both as our privacy policy and as the information notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”). For players in the European Union it also provides the information required by Articles 13 and 14 of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

This notice covers:

  • all versions of the Five Seals game (installed through Google Play or directly),
  • this website and its subpages,
  • any contact you have with us by email.

The Google Play Store, the Android operating system and external websites linked from this site are governed by their own privacy policies. The providers of those services are responsible for how they process your data.

Definitions

  • Personal data: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing: any operation performed on personal data, such as collecting, recording, storing, altering, transferring or erasing it.
  • Data controller: the person who decides the purposes and means of processing; in this notice, Murat Akpınar.
  • Processor: a person or organisation that processes personal data on behalf of and on the instructions of the data controller.
  • Data subject: the natural person whose personal data is processed; that is, you.
  • Local data: files the game creates only on your device and that are never transmitted to us.
  • Cookie: a small text file a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies such as local storage and tracking pixels are treated the same way.

What data is processed?

4.1 The game: data that stays on your device

Five Seals is an offline game. The only permission it requests from Android is vibration; it has no internet permission. The game is therefore technically unable to send any data about you to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, in-app purchases or social features.

To work, the game creates the following files in the app's private internal storage on your device:

RecordContentsSent to us?
Game progresssave.json and backupsRegion, seals, checkpoint, collected items and potion bag; in-game statistics such as play time and number of deaths, kills and parriesNo
Settingssettings.json and backupsLanguage, volume levels, graphics quality, difficulty, button size, opacity and layout, vibration preferenceNo
Save slotsslots/Copies of your progress that you saved manuallyNo
Player profileprofile.jsonWhich tutorial hints you have already seenNo

These files do not contain your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers. On first launch the game reads your device's language setting only to choose the interface language; this information never leaves the device. Gamepad and touch input is processed in the moment and is not recorded.

Android Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up the game files listed above to your Google account so you can move them to a new phone. This backup is performed by Google, under your Google account and subject to Google's privacy terms. We have no access to these backups. You can manage backup under Android Settings › Google › Backup (the menu name may differ by device).

4.2 Downloading through Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, your account, payment and download information is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. The Google Play Console only gives us aggregate statistics such as install counts, country breakdown, ratings and reviews. For users who have turned on “share usage and diagnostics” in their device settings, it also provides crash and “app not responding” reports containing the device model, Android version and error details. These reports do not allow us to identify you as a person and are used only to fix bugs in the game. Reviews you post on Google Play are public; when we reply, we see only the user name that Google displays.

4.3 The website

This is a static website that runs no server-side code and is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, accounts, cookies, local storage, analytics, ads or social media plugins. All images, styles, scripts and fonts are loaded from the site's own server; no request is sent to any third-party server when a page loads.

To display a web page, your browser necessarily sends certain technical information to the server: your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, browser and operating system information (user agent) and the referring address. GitHub states that it logs the IP addresses of visitors to Pages sites for security purposes. These logs are kept by GitHub. We do not access them and do not use them to identify, track or profile visitors.

4.4 When you contact us

If you email us, we process your email address, your name if given, the content of your message and any attachments (for example a screenshot or your device model). Please do not include personal or sensitive information that is not needed.

No special categories of data. The game and the website never process special categories of personal data within the meaning of Article 6 KVKK and Article 9 GDPR, such as health, biometric, religious, ethnic or political data. Location, camera, microphone, contacts and other files on your device are never accessed.

Data handling procedures

We follow the internal procedures below in everything we do with personal data.

6.1 Privacy by design and by default

  • Data minimisation: When a new feature is designed, the first question is “can this work without any data?” Five Seals was built on this principle, without internet permission.
  • Local first: Everything the game needs is kept on the device. A feature that sends data to a server may only be added after this notice has been updated and the change announced to you.
  • Third-party code review: Before any library, SDK or external resource is added to the game or the website, we check whether it collects data. Components that collect data are not added.

6.2 Handling requests

  1. Your request is logged and we confirm that we received it.
  2. Where necessary, we take reasonable steps to verify your identity so that data is not disclosed to the wrong person. We ask only for the minimum information needed for this.
  3. The request is reviewed and concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest (Article 13 KVKK). Under the GDPR the deadline is one month, which may be extended by two further months for complex requests, with reasons given.
  4. If we refuse a request, we tell you in writing why and what remedies are available to you.

6.3 Data breach response

  1. Detection and containment: As soon as a possible breach is noticed, access to the affected systems is restricted and the scope of the breach is determined.
  2. Assessment: We record which data was affected, how many people are concerned and what the risks are.
  3. Notification: Under Article 12(5) KVKK and Decision No. 2019/10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board, the Board is notified within 72 hours at the latest of becoming aware of the breach, and the affected individuals as soon as reasonably possible. Breaches falling under the GDPR are handled under Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.
  4. Remediation and lessons learned: The root cause is fixed, the measures taken are documented and this procedure is updated if needed.

6.4 Records and review

This notice and the related procedures are reviewed at least once a year and before any change to the game or the website that is significant for data processing. Requests, breach assessments and deletion operations are recorded.

Protecting your data

In accordance with Article 12 KVKK and Article 32 GDPR, we take appropriate technical and organisational measures to prevent unlawful processing of and unlawful access to personal data, and to keep it safe.

Technical measures

  • No network access: Because the game does not request internet permission, your data cannot be leaked or intercepted over a network.
  • App sandbox: Save files are kept in Android's app-specific internal storage, which other apps on the device cannot access.
  • Integrity and resilience: Saves are written atomically, validated when opened and protected by backup copies, so your progress is not lost if the app closes suddenly, the battery dies or a file becomes corrupted.
  • Encrypted connection: The website is served over HTTPS; all communication between the site and your browser is encrypted.
  • No external resources: The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or tracking code, so your visit is not shared with other services.
  • Signed packages: Game packages are digitally signed, so you can verify that updates come from us.

Organisational measures

  • Personal data (such as email correspondence) is accessed only by authorised people who need it to answer a request, and only as far as necessary.
  • Publishing, source code and communication accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification.
  • Service providers acting as processors are selected after assessing whether they offer sufficient security guarantees.
  • The procedures in this notice are reviewed regularly.

No system is perfect. If you notice a security vulnerability, please let us know. We welcome responsible disclosure and act on it quickly.

Periodic deletion

Under Article 11 of the Regulation, data whose retention period has expired is deleted in periodic deletion cycles not exceeding 6 months. Erasure, destruction and anonymisation operations are recorded and those records are kept for at least 3 years. Except where retention is required by law, if you ask us as a data subject to delete your data, we will do so within 30 days at the latest.

Deleting your progress yourself

  • In the game: Starting a “New Game” from the main menu deletes the current autosave. You can overwrite the save slots you created manually with a new save.
  • Completely: To delete all game data, including save slots and settings, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. This cannot be undone.

Children's privacy

Five Seals does not ask players for their age and collects no personal data from any player, including children. The age rating appropriate to the game's content is shown in its Google Play rating.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 (or a higher age where the law of their country requires it) has sent us personal data by email without parental consent, we will delete it immediately. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us data, please contact us.

Your rights and how to exercise them

Your rights under Article 11 KVKK

  1. To learn whether your personal data is processed,
  2. to request information about it if it is processed,
  3. to learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose,
  4. to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred,
  5. to request correction if the data is incomplete or inaccurate,
  6. to request erasure or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 KVKK,
  7. to request that correction, erasure or destruction be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  8. to object to a result against you arising from analysis of the data exclusively by automated systems,
  9. to claim compensation for damage suffered as a result of unlawful processing.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the EU, you also have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). If any processing were based on consent, you could withdraw that consent at any time.

A practical note: Because your game data exists only on your own device, you can exercise your rights of access, correction and erasure over it yourself (see section 9). The only data about you that we might hold is the emails you send us.

Right to complain

If your request is refused, you find our answer insufficient or we do not answer in time, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of our answer, and in any case within 60 days of your request (Article 14 KVKK). If you are in the EU, you may also lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence (Article 77 GDPR).
